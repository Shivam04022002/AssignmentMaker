# reference1.png — Lang3 alphabet sheet analysis (development only)

**Source:** `reference/reference1.png`, 960 × 1280 px.

- **What it is:** a phone photo of an A4 sheet lying on a desk. The page is slightly rotated and in perspective, with no ruling.
- **How it was inspected:** at 2.2× (row crops) and 3.5× (letter-group crops).
- **Not bundled:** neither this file nor the reference image is imported by the app or included in the bundle.

## Characters the sheet does not show

The sheet shows no digits, punctuation (apart from one stray dot) or symbols. These keep Lang3's existing single forms:

- digits 0–9;
- `. , : ; ? ! ' " ( ) [ ] { } - – — _ / \ |`;
- `+ = * % & # @ $ ₹ < > ^ ~ • · → ← ° € £ × ÷ ±`.

They were drawn in this hand's proportions in the earlier Lang3 phase. Anything else uses the neutral fallback mark.

## Known approximations

- Reference forms that are tiny or blurred are approximated (marked **A**): the cursive r and s, the C loop, the S loop, and the G entry.
- Two traces were redrawn because a direct trace was unreadable at writing size: the cursive s read as "δ", and the κ-like x read as "k".
- Forms keep each letter's existing width. The sheet's loops are a little wider than Lang3's print letters, so looped forms are slightly compressed.

## Fidelity refinement (follow-up phase)

**Obsolete forms.** These first forms come from the earlier Lang3 page and contradict the sheet. They stay in the glyph table for backward compatibility but have weight 0, so they are never chosen in normal output:

- `f` on the line: every `f` is now a looped `f` below the line;
- the plain-stem `l`: every `l` is now looped;
- the u-shaped `y`: every `y` now has the sheet's loop or hook.

Other first forms were made rarer where the sheet does not show them:

| Form | Weight |
|---|---|
| Plain `a` | 0.15 |
| Footless `i` | 0.3 |
| Open-hook `g` | 0.5 |

**Widths.** Each form takes its own natural width from its body (ink above y 0.25), within 0.85× to +0.35 of the letter's width.

- Entry strokes reach up to 0.06 into the gap before the letter, and exit hooks up to 0.04 into the gap after it. The letter gap is 0.2.
- Example: the looped `l` is 0.50 wide, against the plain stem's 0.24.
- The layout chooses each word's forms from the saved document's seeds and measures the word with them, so line breaks are stable for a saved document.

**No collisions.** Entered forms are not used after a letter that leaves the baseline: the two flicks would meet, a join the sheet does not show. Exit tails keep a 0.22 x-height clearance and are skipped where there is no room ahead.
